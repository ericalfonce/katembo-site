Travel that supports the places you visit

Responsible tourism depends on protecting wildlife, supporting local employment and working with safari partners that operate responsibly.

Conservation, honestly described

Katembo aims to work with guides, accommodation providers and tourism partners whose operations contribute positively to the landscapes and communities on which safari tourism depends.

We only describe specific funding, research or community programmes when we can document them — and we are glad to show guests the chain.

What we do

  • Work with accredited guides, camps and operators, and keep our partner list deliberately short.
  • Prefer private conservancies and community-run concessions where guest money reaches the ground.
  • Arrangement of every community visit stays respectful: no performances on demand, no staged poverty, fair payment for genuine connection.
  • Wildlife first — no crowding, no chasing, no feeding, no off-road pressure to get a shot. The guide decides when a moment is done.

Wildlife ethics

We follow the codes of the Tanzania Wildlife Management Authority and the Kenya Wildlife Service. That means keeping distance, minimising noise, never disturbing den or nest sites, and respecting that the animal always has the right of way — including the right to disappear.

What we ask of you

  • Do not feed, touch or provoke wildlife — in any setting.
  • Leave campsites and trails as you found them; carry out what you carry in.
  • Give generously and cautiously: direct support for vetted community projects does more than random distributions.
  • Ask us where your stay’s money goes — we are glad to show the chain.

The long view

This land has been managed by its people for millennia. Our part is small: guard it, pay for it, and let our guests fall in love with it — because people protect what they love.

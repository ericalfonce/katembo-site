Legal

Terms of Booking

Last updated: September 2026. These are the general terms under which Katembo Safari Ltd. arranges travel. Specific dates, prices and conditions for your journey appear in the proposal we prepare for you and take precedence over these general terms.

Quotes and proposals

Price indications on this site are directional. A personal proposal sets out exact pricing for your dates, camps and flights, and that written figure is the price we hold until the dates you have reserved pass.

Deposit and balance

Reservations are confirmed with a deposit, the amount and deadline of which are stated in your proposal. The balance is due no later than sixty days before travel. Payments are made by bank transfer or the payment channels stated in the proposal.

Changes and cancellations

You may change dates or details any time before the final balance is due, subject to availability and any change fees levied by camps and airlines. Cancellation fees apply from confirmation and scale up as travel approaches, following the policy set out in your proposal. Flights and permits, where issued, are subject to their operators’ own rules.

Traveller obligations

Guests must hold a valid passport, the required visas and any vaccinations for the countries visited. It is entirely your responsibility to be properly documented. Tanzania, Kenya and Rwanda each have their own entry requirements, which we will outline but which you must verify before travel.

On the ground

The wild is unsupervised. Katembo Safari selects accredited operators, camps and guides and requires them to follow strict safety protocols, but neither we nor they can control the behaviour of wildlife. Follow your guide’s instructions at all times.

Liability

Katembo Safari acts as an agent and organiser of travel arrangements supplied by independent providers. To the fullest extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the value of services arranged, and we accept no liability for events beyond reasonable control — weather, political disruption, force majeure or the actions of third-party operators.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania.

An enquiry is not a contract

The wizard and contact forms on this site create no booking. Only a signed written proposal and your confirmed deposit form a contract.

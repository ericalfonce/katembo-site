Introduction

Your safari, planned around you

Katembo Safari is a Tanzania-based safari company creating private and tailor-made journeys across East Africa.

We bring together experienced guides, carefully selected camps and lodges, private safari vehicles, regional flights and on-the-ground support to create smooth, personal journeys from arrival to departure.

Whether you are travelling for the Great Migration, a family safari, a honeymoon, photography or a safari-and-beach escape, we shape the itinerary around your interests, travel dates and pace.

Experiences

Safari experiences

A safari is more than game drives.

Depending on the destination and season, your journey may include walking safaris, balloon flights, cultural visits, photographic safaris, bush dining, hiking, boat safaris and time on the Indian Ocean coast.

Our team helps place each experience where it makes the most sense within your itinerary.

Conservation

Travel that supports the places you visit

Responsible tourism depends on protecting wildlife, supporting local employment and working with safari partners that operate responsibly.

Katembo aims to work with guides, accommodation providers and tourism partners whose operations contribute positively to the landscapes and communities on which safari tourism depends.

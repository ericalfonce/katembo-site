Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy concerns the static website of Katembo Safari Ltd. and takes its lead from Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act (2022) and, where applicable, the GDPR for guests visiting from the European Union.

What we collect, and why

We collect the minimum needed to plan a journey or reply to a message:

  • Enquiry wizard and contact form — name, email, phone, travel preferences and any message you choose to share. Used solely to prepare your tailored safari proposal.
  • Newsletter — the email address you volunteer, used only to send Field Notes. You may unsubscribe at any time by replying.

How your data is handled

This website is static and stores no visitor data on our servers. Enquiries reach us by email as written message text. Once an enquiry becomes a booking, your details are held in our internal records for the duration of the travel planning and for the period required by Tanzanian law.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share only what a journey requires — for example passenger names and passport details with airlines, camps and immigration systems, always limited to what those operators need.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to journeys@katembosafari.com and we will respond within thirty days.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies and uses no third-party analytics scripts.

Questions

For any privacy concern, contact our data custodian at journeys@katembosafari.com.

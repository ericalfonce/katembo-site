A Tanzania Safari Company

Katembo Safari is a Tanzania-based safari company creating private and tailor-made journeys across Tanzania and East Africa — planned around your interests, travel dates and pace.

Our approach

Travel, planned around you

The finest journey in Africa is not the longest, the fastest or the one with the largest list of sights — it is the one planned around you. Katembo exists to give travellers exactly that: a private safari vehicle, a dedicated guide, camps and lodges selected with local knowledge, and days shaped to your own pace.

We work at a personal scale on purpose. The same planner begins and ends your journey, the same guide travels with you day after day, and the stays we recommend are chosen for how well they suit your itinerary and the way you like to travel.

How we work

Four commitments

01

One planner, end to end

The person who designs your journey is the person, by email and phone, who answers when you write from camp.

02

Only what fits

Every camp and lodge we recommend is chosen for its location, character and how well it suits your journey — not simply the rate.

03

Private by default

Your own vehicle and guide unless you ask otherwise. The safari belongs to your party alone.

04

Honest about the wild

Wildlife is not a promise. We plan around seasons and the herds, and we would rather under-promise than oversell.

Where we are

Based in Tanzania

Katembo works from Arusha and Nairobi — inside the region, not distant from it. That proximity is the core of our approach: same season, same rains, same long-standing relationships with camps and communities.

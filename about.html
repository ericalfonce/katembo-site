About Katembo

A Small House, Devoted

Katembo Safari is a deliberately small East African safari house. We take a limited number of guests each season, and treat each journey as the only one that matters.

Our belief

Travel, composed

The finest journey in Africa is not the longest, the fastest or the most crowded of accolades — it is the one arranged entirely around you. Katembo exists to give travellers exactly that: a private 4x4, a dedicated guide, camps chosen with personal knowledge, and no point in the journey shared with strangers unless you wish it.

We are small on purpose. Fewer guests means the same planner begins and ends your journey, the same guide reads your moods on day one, and the camps you stay in are the ones we would book our own families into.

How we work

Four quiet promises

01

One planner, end to end

The person who designs your journey is the person, by email and phone, who answers when you write from camp.

02

Only what we know

Every camp and lodge we recommend has been stood in by us. If we would not send our own family, we do not send you.

03

Private by default

Your own vehicle and guide unless you ask otherwise. The safari belongs to your party alone.

04

Honest about the wild

Wildlife is not a promise. We plan around seasons and the herds, and we would rather under-promise than oversell.

Where we are

Based in the land we sell

Katembo works from Arusha and Nairobi — inside the region, not distant from it. That proximity is the core of our approach: same season, same rains, same long-standing relationships with camps and communities.
